🔧 Technical Enhancements

1. Multi-scale Unsharp Mask

Applies sharpening at multiple sigma levels (0.5, 1.0, 2.0) to preserve both fine details AND broader structures.

2. Edge-Aware Sharpening

Uses Sobel edge detection to apply stronger sharpening ONLY on textured regions, avoiding noise amplification in flat areas.

3. Detail Injection from Original

Extracts high-frequency details from the LR image and injects them back into SR output for better texture preservation.

4. Contrast-Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization (CLAHE)

Improves local contrast without over-amplifying noise in homogeneous regions.
