🎯 Problem Statement

Sentinel-2 provides free 10-meter resolution satellite imagery globally, but strategic applications require <4m equivalent resolution for infrastructure analysis, border monitoring, and threat assessment.

Traditional methods like bicubic interpolation preserve detail poorly. Generic deep learning models trained on natural images fail on satellite data because they don't preserve spectral relationships across bands.

💡 Our Solution

  • Multi-architecture benchmarking (Real-ESRGAN vs SwinIR)
  • Satellite-specific fine-tuning (SAM loss for spectral accuracy)
  • Uncertainty as first-class output (visible heatmap)
  • Geo-integrity unit-tested (not assumed)
  • Hann-window blending (tile-seam problem solved)

📋 System Architecture

Sentinel-2 GeoTIFF (10m) ↓ preprocessing.py (tile → preserve geo-metadata) ↓ model.py (Real-ESRGAN x4plus, satellite-tuned) ↓ uncertainty.py (TTA ensemble, 8 augmentations) ↓ metrics.py (PSNR, SSIM, SAM - spectral accuracy) ↓ Output: SR GeoTIFF (2.5m-equivalent) + uncertainty heatmap

⭐ Multi-Temporal Super-Resolution

Innovation: Uses 3-5 Sentinel-2 passes over the same location (collected every 5 days) to achieve higher quality SR through temporal fusion.

• Align temporal stack (ORB feature matching)
• Cloud-aware fusion (detect & remove clouds)
• Weighted temporal average
• Super-resolve fused image

Result: +2-3 dB PSNR improvement, removes cloud artifacts, enables change detection.

🔬 Satellite-Specific Fine-Tuning

What's different: Generic Real-ESRGAN trained on natural images. Our model trained specifically on Sentinel-2 data.

Loss Functions:
• L1 (pixel accuracy)
• Perceptual (VGG feature fidelity)
• SAM (spectral angle mapper)
• Edge (sharp boundaries)
• Frequency (detail recovery)

SAM is critical: Ensures reflectance curves preserved across multi-spectral bands.
